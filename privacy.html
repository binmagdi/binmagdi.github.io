Privacy Policy

Youtube Studio Dashboard · Last updated 8 November 2026

Youtube Studio Dashboard is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps the owner follow the numbers of the YouTube channels they run. It is not offered to the public, and only the owner signs in to it.

What it reads

When the owner signs in with Google and allows access, the tool reads, for the owner's own channels only:

  • the channel's name and ID, to know which channel was connected (YouTube Data API, read-only);
  • analytics for the channel's own videos: views, watch time, average view duration and percentage, subscribers gained and lost, audience retention and traffic sources (YouTube Analytics API, read-only).

It asks only for the read-only scopes youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. It cannot upload, edit, delete or comment on anything.

Where the data stays

The data and the sign-in token are saved only on the owner's computer, in the tool's own folder. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs, nothing is sold or shared with anyone, and nothing is used for advertising.

Google API Services

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access page, or with the "disconnect" button in the tool, which deletes the saved token.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
